# Privacy Policy — Mission Control (Envogue pilot)

Effective: 2026-08-26. This is the pilot privacy notice for the Mission Control
marketing-operations app operated by Empyrean Consult on behalf of the Envogue
pilot.

## What we store

- **Account data** — name, email, and a hashed password (bcrypt) when you
  create an account. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
- **Workspace data** — campaigns you create (title, brief, channel), scheduled
  sends and their delivery status, and AI usage records (provider, model,
  token counts) attributed to your workspace.
- **AI prompts** — text you send to the AI assistant is forwarded to the AI
  provider (DeepSeek) to generate drafts; generated images use Google Gemini
  (GOOGLE_API_KEY — Gemini 2.5 Flash Image for drafts, gemini-3-pro-image for
  hero shots). Prompts are processed by those providers under their own
  policies.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not use tracking cookies; the app uses a session cookie for
  authentication and a local preference for theme choice.

## Data deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account from the dashboard (**Delete
account**). That permanently removes the account, its workspace, campaigns,
scheduled sends and AI usage rows. You can also email the owner
(team@empyrean.co.za) and we will do the same.
